# Code of conduct

Tacet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that do not follow this.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email link on [mazenzwin.com](https://www.mazenzwin.com). Reports are kept confidential.
